Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

Confetti & Co. Paper Shop ("I," "me," "the shop") runs confettiandcopapershop.com. This page explains what information the site collects, why, and what choices you have. It's written in plain English because it's a small, one-person shop.

What I collect

When you join the email list

If you sign up for the free printable, I collect your first name and email address (and your last name, if you choose to give it). Signups are handled by Mailchimp, which stores the list on my behalf. I use it only to send you the free printable, your welcome discount code, new product announcements, and party ideas, a few times a month at most.

When you browse the site

The site may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use it: which pages are visited, roughly where visitors are located, what device and browser they use, and which links they click (for example, which products send people to Etsy). Google Analytics uses cookies to do this. The information is aggregated. I can't see who you are from it.

When you buy

This site doesn't sell anything directly. All purchases happen on Etsy, and Etsy's own privacy policy covers your order, payment, and account information. I never see your payment details.

What I don't do

  • I don't sell, rent, or trade your email address or any personal information.
  • I don't send your information to advertisers.
  • I don't collect payment information on this site.

Third-party services

The site links to and loads content from a few outside services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Etsy: the shop, checkout, and product images shown on this site
  • Mailchimp (Intuit): the email list
  • Google Analytics: site traffic statistics
  • Pinterest: if you follow a link to the shop's Pinterest profile

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical information (like IP addresses) for security purposes.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe anytime using the link at the bottom of every email.
  • Ask me to delete your information by sending a message through my Etsy shop.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's Analytics opt-out add-on.

Children

This site is meant for adults planning parties. I don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update the date at the top of this page.

Questions

Message me anytime through my Etsy shop.
