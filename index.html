About the shop

Printable joy, made by one person who really likes a good party

Welcome to Confetti & Co. Paper Shop. I create ready-to-print games, signs, and party printables that make hosting easier and a little more fun. Quick questions

Do I need special software to fill these in?

No — just free Adobe Acrobat Reader (Windows, Mac, iPhone, or Android). Open the PDF, click into the highlighted fields, and type. No design skills, no account, no app to buy.

What if I don't have Acrobat installed?

It's a free download from Adobe and takes under a minute to install — every order includes a direct link and a quick how-to guide.

Can I still use it if I'd rather not type into fields?

Yes — every game also prints blank so you can fill it in by hand.

What do I actually receive?

An instant digital download: the fillable PDF plus a short how-to guide. No physical item ships, so there's nothing to wait on.
