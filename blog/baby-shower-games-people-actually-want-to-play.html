5 Baby Shower Games People Actually Want to Play

no awkward icebreakers required

Most baby shower games fall into one of two camps: the kind everyone quietly dreads (measuring the mom-to-be's belly with yarn) or the kind that actually gets a room of mixed-generation, mixed-energy guests laughing together. Here are five in the second camp — gender-neutral, easy to print, and built to fill the gaps between opening gifts without dragging.

1. Baby Bingo, played during gift-opening

Give every guest a bingo card of likely shower-gift items (onesies, diapers, a stuffed animal, anything monogrammed) before the gifts start. They mark off squares as the mom-to-be opens each present — it keeps everyone watching instead of scrolling their phone, and someone always yells "bingo" at the exact right moment.

2. Guess the Baby Food, blind-tasted

Peel the labels off a handful of baby food jars, number them, and have guests guess the flavor by taste (or smell, for the squeamish). It's a two-minute game with a guaranteed laugh — nobody guesses "butternut squash" correctly on the first try.

3. Word Scramble with a nursery theme

A short list of baby-related words scrambled into puzzles doubles as a quiet activity for guests to work on before the main games start, especially useful if people trickle in over 15–20 minutes.

4. Advice & Wishes Cards instead of a guest book

Skip the traditional guest book and hand out fillable advice cards instead — one line for parenting advice, one for a wish for the baby. They read out loud beautifully during a lull, and the parents keep the stack afterward instead of a book that gets shelved.

5. Guess the Price, for the gift-savvy crowd

Guests guess the retail price of a few baby items on display; closest guess without going over wins. It's a light, low-prep game that works well as a filler between bigger activities.

The common thread: none of these need props you don't already have, and every one keeps the whole room involved instead of putting the mom-to-be on the spot for twenty minutes straight.
